# Provider: InnerTubeX (library)

Source of findings: [docs/spikes/2026-10-innertubex.md](../spikes/2026-10-innertubex.md), read 2026-10-30 from public pages; nothing compiled at that time.

- Tier: 2 (client library for unofficial YouTube endpoints)
- Endpoint: none of its own; it is the transport for the [YouTube Music](youtube-music.md) record. Distributed as `com.github.MetrolistGroup.innertubex:innertubex` through JitPack, pinned at `v0.5.2`.
- Owner: MetrolistGroup (`MetrolistGroup/innertubex`); no relationship with Aria
- License: GPL-3.0. The repository page does not say whether the grant is `-only` or `-or-later`, and the LICENSE text and source headers have not been read, so the variant is unconfirmed. GPL-3.0 is compatible with Aria's `GPL-3.0-only` either way.
- Store policy: JitPack is listed among trusted repositories in the F-Droid Inclusion Policy (the binary must also be freely licensed, which holds). The IzzyOnDroid policy does not mention JitPack or other dependency repositories; its rules are no proprietary components and no tracking or advertising. Residual risk: F-Droid may ask for a reproducible build of a JitPack artifact. Record any such request here.
- PoToken: not generated by the library. It exposes a `TokenProvider` interface; Meld generates tokens with a WebView-based generator in its app code. Aria has no generator yet. This is a known gap for sub-project 2 (playback); a port needs its own ledger row and license check.
- Default: on (required by the YouTube Music provider)
- Kill switch: the YouTube Music kill switch covers it
- Fallback: none; the same as the YouTube Music record
- Data sent: none of its own; it is the transport for the YouTube Music record
- When: whenever the YouTube Music record applies
- Opt-in: not applicable
- Version pinning: the README says `0.x` releases are experimental; read the CHANGELOG before upgrading. Pinned by checksum once `gradle/verification-metadata.xml` exists (open gate in [release-gates.md](../release-gates.md)).
